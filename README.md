One funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its human, under 200 characters, keeping the deadpan shape of a real review while the complaint goes somewhere only a cat would go. An AI judge compares the lines two at a time in three private blind rounds, and the funniest lines take the ten paid places of the $2 pool.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` writes the entry deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`, self-checks the gate (one line, 1–200 characters by code-point count, no control characters, no edge whitespace), reads the file back and verifies it byte-for-byte.

## The entry

> One star. He closes the bedroom door at night, then acts shocked when I scream through it at 3 a.m. The door is my enemy. He is its accomplice.
